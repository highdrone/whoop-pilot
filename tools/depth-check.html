Live depth and change check

Runs the app's live depth (vision/depth.js: Depth Anything V2 Small in its worker, aligned to the twin) and change detector (nav/changes.js) on the splat twin of a capture served under /house-files/. Query: &project= (a capture folder name; default the newest), &model=dav2s|dav2s-fp16, &seconds= (patrol length, 60), &only=load,align,change,door,approach,goggles, &vox=0 (no 3D map), &nobody=1 (the quiet patrol without the person standing about), &cache=1 (keep the 3D map in OPFS), &approachErr= (the share nav/avoid.js takes a new thing's depth to be off by; default AVOID.depthErr), &brake= (how hard the drone really brakes in the approach, a share of what the cap assumes; 0.7), &light=shadow|lamp|night (the house lit unlike the capture), &people=0|only (skip, or run only, the patrol with people the detector misses), &cast=stand|sit (only one of them), &save= (an address that takes POST /save?name= for the screenshots), &augment=1|nolens (the O4 video: blur, exposure, noise, JPEG, OSD, and a lens error unless nolens), &poseErr= (m the localized pose wanders), &zErr= (m the localized height is off the whole way), &zSigma= (the height σ the frames say), &dump= (an address that takes POST /dump?run=&i= for every frame).
