AI survey check: the real capture's twin, a fake Claude

Imports a capture through the server (/house-files/, newest first, or ?project=ID), renders the survey's views with the splat twin (level, and down at the floor or up at the ceiling where a level camera misses them), answers each room's request with a canned Claude that boxes the capture's own objects (RoomPlan furniture, the fan, curtains, stairs) as a model would without the scan's depth (their 3D boxes projected, edges jittered), and lifts those boxes to 3D with the twin's depth. The map compares where the boxes landed (dots) with the objects' footprints (outlines), and shows the floor (red) and ceiling (violet) no view saw. Nothing is sent anywhere. Optional: &rooms=r1,r2 &jitter=0.1 &sink=URL (posts the pictures as PNG).
