Wave C integration: the whole stack flying a patrol in the house twin

Runs under tools/whoop.mjs (the capture comes from /house-files/). Wires what main.js will: the voxel map, the simulator in the splat twin (augmented, lens error), splat localization (fused), live depth, avoidance and change detection, the flight memory, Claude's checks (a fake Claude: no key, no network) and the mission runner; then flies patrols for &seconds=90 of flight with a box and a closed door added mid-flight and a person walking about. Query: &capture=project id &seconds=90 &det=detector|truth &cache=1 (reuse the stored voxels and position database) &person=0 (nobody about) &rate=0.33 (the simulated world at a third of the wall clock's speed, so the GPU keeps up as on a faster machine; 1: real time, the window visible) &seed=11 &save=URL (POST /save?name=c-int-*: pictures and results; &tag=c-int-sealed-1 names them) &scenario=sealed (fly to &from=Living room, then every doorway into &room=Room 3 is closed: look into it, then patrol; both must say it couldn't be checked, touching nothing) &load=4.2 (the machine's load average, kept with the result) &claudeConf=0.7 (how sure the fake Claude is that a suspected change is no change; return home passes one at 0.8 or more) &trace=1 (every control tick in the air: the setpoint through each safety layer, the truth and the estimate) &askfix=0 (vision relocalizes only when lost, as nav/splatloc.js does until it reads the localizer's needFix()). A run whose simulator camera falls back to the simple one, or with no vision fix in its first 10 s of flight, stops and fails. Also checked: the height in the air (p95 under 0.12 m, 97% within 2.5 σz), with the vision fixes' own height error; the door readings are in result.flight.scene. window.result holds it all.
