3D map check

Builds the house's voxel map (app/js/house/voxels.js) from a capture the server serves under /house-files/: splat surfaces, the capture camera's rays, the twin's depth cubes at the capture positions and a frontier sweep of virtual drone viewpoints, then the coverage report (coverage.js), and checks the twin's props and depthBatch on the same splat. Query: ?project= (default: the newest capture), &views= (frontier views, 300), &save= (URL that takes POSTed PNGs: ?name=c-map3d-...), &only=build,changes,live,props, &keep=1 (keep the twin afterwards: window.ctx for console experiments). "live" flies the twin's depth along a planned path with noise and pose error through integrate() and times it with the next map query; "changes" closes and opens every door and confirms an obstacle over never-seen space and then its removal, and checks the map is as it was.
